About

A bit about me.

I'm Matt, a developer and designer. Most of my time goes into banners and graphics and custom Discord bots. On the side I take on websites and other one-off projects, usually because they sounded fun.

I like work that's quick to understand and nice to use. A banner should read at a glance. A bot should do its job without anyone needing a guide. A website should feel obvious the first time you open it.

Most people find me through Discord, and that's still the best way to reach me. I keep a public order queue so you always know where your project stands.

Questions

Things people ask.

How do I start a project?

Message me on Discord with what you need, any examples you like, and when you need it by. I'll reply with questions or a rough plan.

How long does it take?

Depends on the job and how busy the queue is. Graphics are usually quick; bots and side projects take longer. You can watch your order move on the queue page.

Can I see progress?

Yes. Every order goes on the public queue (your name stays hidden unless you're fine with it), and I send previews as I go.

Do you do revisions?

Yes. Tell me what's off and I'll fix it. If the brief changes a lot halfway through, we'll talk about it first.

Do you take work outside those three things?

Sometimes. Ask. If I'm not the right person, I'll tell you.
